Parallelism

Programming models many tasks at once · workers sum 0

Parallelism means doing many calculations simultaneously—typically on separate cores or machines. Split an array, sum each slice on its own worker, then combine. That differs from concurrency, where tasks may only interleave on one core. Parallel workers often still need synchronization at the merge step.
