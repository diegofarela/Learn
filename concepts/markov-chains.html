Markov chains

Probability for CS states · P · stationary t = 0

A Markov chain is a random walk on states where the next step depends only on the present—not the full past (memoryless). PageRank, MCMC, and many CS109 examples are chains. Needs conditional probability and matrix intuition from linear algebra.

When this is useful

  • PageRank-like rankings, weather-ish UI models, or user session step flows.
  • Speech/NLP emission models and simple generative text toys.
  • Reliability models: probability a service is up after n failover transitions.
