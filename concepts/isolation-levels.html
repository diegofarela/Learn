Isolation levels

Databases dirty · nonrepeatable · phantom level RC

Isolation levels trade concurrency for which anomalies concurrent transactions may see: dirty reads, nonrepeatable reads, phantoms. Stricter levels block more. Related: transactions and concurrency.
