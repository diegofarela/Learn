Probability basics

Probability for CS sample space · event · independence P = 0.5

Probability assigns numbers in [0,1] to events inside a sample space. Independence lets you multiply—key for hashing, balls & bins, and expectation. Counting outcomes often uses combinatorics.

Sample space & events

        

          Ω is the set of outcomes. An event A ⊆ Ω; for a uniform finite space,
          P(A) = |A| / |Ω|. Highlighting cells is literally measuring that
          fraction.
        


        
Independence

        

          Coin flips are independent: knowing the first is H does not change
          P(second = H). Then P(A ∩ B) = P(A)P(B). Dependence breaks that
          product rule.
