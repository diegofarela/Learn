Priority queues

Data structures ADT · enqueue by priority · extract max / min size 4

A priority queue is an ADT: each item carries a priority, and you always serve the most important one next (highest or lowest, depending on the convention). A plain queue is FIFO; a priority queue ignores arrival order when priorities differ. Under the hood it is often a binary heap (the priority tree—not the memory-allocation heap).
