Compilers & interpreters

Systems lex · parse · codegen stage source

A compiler turns source text into a lower form (bytecode or machine code) through stages: lexing, parsing, then codegen. An interpreter may walk the AST or bytecode at run time instead. Related: call stack (runtime frames) and recursion (AST walks).
