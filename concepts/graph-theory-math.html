Definitions

math 

            

              Connectivity is an equivalence relation on V.
            

          

        


        
Paths

        

          A path is a walk with no repeated vertices. Distance is the fewest
          edges in a path—what BFS computes on unweighted graphs.
        


        
Cuts

        

          A cut partitions V into S and V−S; the cut-set is edges with one end
          in each side. Min-cuts appear in network flow and cluster boundaries.
        


        
Connected components

        

          In an undirected graph, components are the maximal connected
          subgraphs. Removing a bridge can split one component into two—prove
          it with
          induction
          on edges.
