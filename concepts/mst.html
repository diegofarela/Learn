Minimum spanning trees

Algorithms Kruskal · no cycles · union-find MST edges 0/5

A minimum spanning tree connects every vertex of a weighted undirected graph with the cheapest possible total edge weight and no cycles. Kruskal sorts edges lightest-first and adds one only if its ends are in different components—tracked with union-find. Same idea as other greedy graph algorithms.

Cheapest safe edge

        

          Sort every edge by weight. Walk that list: if the two endpoints are
          already connected (same union-find set), adding the edge would create a
          cycle—skip it. Otherwise accept it and union the two
          components. Stop after V − 1 edges; the accepted edges are an MST.
