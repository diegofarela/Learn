Failures & timeouts

Distributed crash · partition · timeout · retry idle

In a network you cannot tell a slow node from a dead one—so you set a timeout, then retry or fail over. A partition can look the same from one side. That pairs with CAP trade-offs, idempotent retries, and reliable transport ideas in TCP.
