Networks are built as a stack: each layer offers a service to the one above and uses the one below. Toggle TCP/IP (4) or OSI (7), click a layer for its job. Application traffic includes HTTP and DNS.

Why layers

        

          Separating concerns lets HTTP ignore Ethernet details and IP ignore
          browser quirks. TCP/IP is the practical Internet model; OSI is a
          finer teaching map that still maps cleanly onto it.
