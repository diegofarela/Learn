TLS & HTTPS

Networks handshake · padlock · HTTPS step 0/5

TLS encrypts a TCP connection after a handshake that agrees on keys and authenticates the server. HTTPS is just HTTP riding that tunnel—same methods and status codes, private on the wire. Contrast plaintext HTTP below; related: encryption, TCP, sockets.
