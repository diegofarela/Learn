Online algorithms

Algorithms ski rental · competitive · OPT day 0

An online algorithm must decide without seeing the future. The classic ski rental puzzle: rent for $1/day or buy for $B — but you don’t know how many days you’ll ski. Compare your policy to an offline optimum that knew the length in advance (the competitive ratio). Cost language ties to Big-O and greedy local choices.

When this is useful

  • Streaming ads or recommendations as events arrive—no full dataset upfront.
  • Caching eviction (LRU-style) and real-time bidding under partial information.
  • Live dashboards that update decisions as each click/order lands.
