Concurrency

Programming models interleaving · many tasks in progress step 0

Concurrency means dealing with many tasks at once—their progress can interleave on one core via time-slicing. That is not the same as parallelism, where work truly runs simultaneously on multiple cores. Concurrency often uses threads and needs synchronization when they share state.
