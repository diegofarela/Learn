CPU scheduling

Systems FCFS · Round Robin · quantum avg wait —

The scheduler picks which ready process (or thread) runs next on the CPU. FCFS runs jobs to completion in arrival order; Round Robin slices time into a quantum so no job monopolizes the CPU. Both feed the same ready queue idea—different selection rules.

FCFS

        

          First-come, first-served is simple and fair in arrival
          order, but a long job at the head can make short jobs wait
          (convoy effect).
        


        
Round Robin

        

          Each job gets up to q time units, then goes to the back of the
          ready queue. Smaller q feels more interactive; larger
          q approaches FCFS with less context-switch overhead.
