Information & entropy

Probability for CS entropy · bits · surprise H — bits

Entropy measures average surprise of a random source— how many fair-coin bits you need in the ideal case. It shows up in compression, decision trees, and ML feature criteria. Built from probability and logging unexpected events.

When this is useful

  • Compression features, entropy of passwords, and “how surprising is this event?”
  • ML feature usefulness and decision-tree splits grounded in information gain.
  • Designing codes/protocols that fit more signal into limited bandwidth.
