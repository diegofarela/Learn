Decidability

Theory halting · undecidable · postcard trace 0

Some yes/no questions about programs have no algorithm that always answers correctly and finishes. The classic example is the halting problem: will this program stop, or loop forever? You can simulate for a while — but “forever” is not something a finite tracer can certify. Related hardness ideas live in P vs NP.
