Variance & concentration

Probability for CS Var · σ · Chebyshev σ —

Variance measures spread around the mean; concentration says sample averages settle near expectation. Chebyshev needs only variance; Chernoff/Hoeffding give sharper exponential tails when variables are bounded—standard CS109-style tools.

Variance

        

          Var(X) = E[(X−μ)²] = E[X²]−μ². For independent summands,
          variances add. Standard deviation σ = √Var.
        

        
Chebyshev

        

          P(|X−μ| ≥ t) ≤ Var(X)/t². Weak but assumption-light—enough
          to prove weak laws of large numbers. Sharper tools exist when you have
          boundedness or moment-generating functions.
        

        

          Used in hashing load,
          balls & bins,
          and randomized algorithm analysis.
