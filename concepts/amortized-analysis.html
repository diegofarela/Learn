Amortized analysis

Algorithms aggregate · accounting · potential ops 0

A single array append can be cheap — or copy the whole buffer when it doubles. Amortized analysis spreads expensive rare steps over a sequence so the amortized cost stays low. Related growth language: Big-O.

When this is useful

  • Explaining why a dynamic array/ArrayList append is “usually O(1)” in your mobile app.
  • Sizing caches and hash tables that occasionally resize under traffic spikes.
  • Justifying a data-structure choice in a design doc when worst-case ≠ typical case.
