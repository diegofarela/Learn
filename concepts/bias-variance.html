Bias–variance

ML foundations underfit · overfit · tradeoff deg 1

Bias is error from a too-simple model (underfit); variance is sensitivity to the particular sample (overfit). Slide polynomial degree on noisy points to see the tradeoff. Fits use ideas from linear algebra; training often uses gradient descent.
