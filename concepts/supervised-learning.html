Supervised learning

ML foundations labels · loss · generalization degree 2

Supervised learning fits a model from input→label pairs, then predicts on new inputs. Choose a hypothesis class, a loss, and optimize (often with gradient descent). Bias–variance explains under/overfit.

The loop

        

          Split data → fit on train → evaluate on held-out test. Classification
          vs regression is about label type; both are supervised.
        

        
Loss & regularization

        

          Loss measures train fit; regularization (weight decay, early stop)
          trades train loss for better generalization—same story as high
          variance in bias–variance.
