TCP & UDP

Networks reliable stream · datagram · handshake step 0/6

TCP opens a connection, numbers segments, and retransmits until delivery is ordered and complete. UDP just ships datagrams—fast, no handshake, no guarantees. Apps sit on sockets; the web’s HTTP usually rides TCP.
