Assembly basics

Architecture registers · MOV · ADD · CALL / RET PC 0

Assembly is a human-readable form of machine instructions: move data between registers and memory, do arithmetic, and CALL/RET for subroutines. It sits under high-level languages and beside the instruction cycle that fetches each opcode—see also the call stack (not the stack ADT page alone).
