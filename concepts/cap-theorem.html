CAP theorem

Databases consistency · availability · partition tolerance pick 2/3

In a partitioned network you can’t keep full consistency and full availability at once. Systems choose a corner of the triangle—relevant when comparing relational / ACID stores with many NoSQL designs.

Pick 2 of 3

CA / CP / AP

Toggle properties — invalid “all three” is blocked under partition

CA — fine without a network split.

Toggle C / A / P. Under partition, you may keep only two.
