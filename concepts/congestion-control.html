Congestion control

Networks cwnd · AIMD · AIMD cwnd 1

Congestion control keeps senders from flooding the path—about shared network capacity, not backpressure inside one app alone. TCP’s classic story: grow the congestion window (cwnd) additively, cut it multiplicatively when loss signals overload—the AIMD “sawtooth.” Built on TCP; loss shows up as missing ACKs on the transport layer.
