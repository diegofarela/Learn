Conditional probability answers “given that B happened, how likely is A?” Bayes’ rule flips evidence and hypothesis—the backbone of spam filters, medical tests, and ML inference. Builds on probability basics and pairs with expectation.

Definition

        

          P(A|B) = P(A∩B) / P(B) when P(B) > 0. Independence means
          P(A|B) = P(A).
        

        
Bayes’ rule

        

          P(H|E) = P(E|H) P(H) / P(E). The denominator expands by
          total probability. Base rates matter: a positive test for a rare event
          is often still mostly false positives.
