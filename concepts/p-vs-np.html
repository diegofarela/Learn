P vs NP

Theory classes · reductions · hardness focus map

P is problems solvable in polynomial time; NP is problems whose yes-answers have short, checkable certificates. Whether P = NP is open. We relate problems with reductions — polynomial maps that preserve yes/no. Growth rates from Big-O measure a specific algorithm; decidability asks whether any algorithm exists at all.

When this is useful

  • Spotting NP-hard scheduling/routing asks that need approximations in a logistics app.
  • Explaining why brute-force seat assignment won’t scale to festival size.
  • Choosing heuristics/ML when exact optimization is intractable.
