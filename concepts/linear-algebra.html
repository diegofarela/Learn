Linear algebra

ML foundations vectors · matrices · transform ∥v∥ —

Vectors are arrows (or lists of numbers); matrices transform them—scale, rotate, shear. In ML these are the geometry of features and parameters, not array ADTs by another name. Drag and tweak below; these ops underpin gradient descent and model geometry in bias–variance fits. Spectral structure: eigenvalues & SVD.

When this is useful

  • Embeddings, image filters, and recommendation vectors under the hood of ML features.
  • Game graphics transforms and camera math in an engine.
  • Ranking/search systems that score documents with matrix operations.
