Transactions & ACID

Databases BEGIN · COMMIT · ROLLBACK step 0/5

A transaction groups updates into one all-or-nothing unit. COMMIT makes them durable; ROLLBACK undoes them. ACID names the guarantees relational engines aim for—contrast with many NoSQL trade-offs.
